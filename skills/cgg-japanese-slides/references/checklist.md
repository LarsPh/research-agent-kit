# Pre-review checklist

Run on the exported PDF, page by page. Letters refer to sections of [rules](rules.md).

1. Any text under 20pt, page numbers and citations included? Any item over one line? (A)
2. Line breaks at meaningful points, in English too? (A)
3. A one-sentence message at the top left, matching the title and the whole page? Subject named? (B)
4. Every task, role, and key term explained before it is used, and each page following from the last? (B)
5. Explanations written on the figure, next to their targets? No legends or distant notes? (C)
6. Nothing at the edges or in the bottom band? Margins balanced? No overlaps or overflow? Centres aligned? (C)
7. Background figures show the rendering process only? Hard concepts have a figure? (D)
8. Arrowheads large and on target? Rays look 3D? One camera icon? Dashed lines visible? (D)
9. Metrics with ↑↓, units separate, ○✕ prominent, highlights explained on screen? (E)
10. Captions below figures; 「図の出典: 」 for others' figures only, without figure numbers? (F)
11. 体言止め, half-width brackets and colons, spaces between half- and full-width characters, 「・」 on all
    bullets? (G)
12. Standard terms and words that mean what is intended (再構成, 同一性, 問題点 / 課題)? (G)
13. Each element appears only when explained, in order? No click that dumps a lot at once? (H)
14. Cover order and wording, own name highlighted, outlook at field level, no closing thank-you page? (I)
